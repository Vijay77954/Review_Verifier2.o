Key Problem Area

⭐

1. Inflated Ratings

High rating products often use robot ratings or paid reviewers to artificially inflate their scores, deceiving honest buyers.

💵

2. Deceptive Tactics

Money in exchange for 5-star reviews is a common practice, making the rating system unreliable and biased.

🤔

3. Customer Misleading

Users looking for quality products end up confused and misled by high ratings on poor quality items, wasting their money.

Our Solution

We built a simple website interface where customers paste any product URL to immediately receive a detailed fake review analysis and authenticity score.

Our tool identifies suspicious patterns, bot activity, and unusual language in reviews to calculate a true adjusted rating, saving you time and money.
